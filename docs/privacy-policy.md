## 中文版

「网页自动截屏助手」(以下简称"本扩展")是一款 Chrome 浏览器扩展,用于定时或手动截取网页截图并保存到您的电脑。**本扩展不收集、不传输、不出售任何用户数据。**

### 1. 我们收集哪些数据

**无。** 本扩展没有任何分析统计、广告、追踪或遥测代码,不向开发者的任何服务器发送请求。所有功能均在您的设备本地完成。

### 2. 本扩展访问了哪些内容

- **网页截图:** 当您手动或按设定间隔触发截屏时,本扩展通过浏览器提供的截屏接口获取当前网页画面,并**直接保存到您电脑的下载目录**。截图全程不离开您的设备,开发者无法接触。
- **页面尺寸与滚动:** 整页长图模式下,注入的脚本会读取页面高度并滚动页面,仅用于拼接截图,不读取、不上传页面内容本身。
- **标签页信息:** 读取标签页的网址和标题,仅用于生成截图文件名,不做任何记录或上传。
- **设置数据:** 您的偏好设置(间隔、格式、命名规则等)保存在 `chrome.storage` 中。若您开启了 Chrome 自身的同步功能,设置可能经由您的 Google 账号同步,该过程由 [Google 的隐私政策](https://policies.google.com/privacy)管辖,开发者无法访问这些数据。

### 3. 数据的存储与保留

截图保存于您本地的下载文件夹,由您完全掌控,可随时自行删除。卸载本扩展会自动清除其保存的设置。开发者不保留任何用户数据。

### 4. 第三方与数据出售

本扩展不与任何第三方共享数据,不包含第三方 SDK,不出售数据,不将数据用于与核心功能无关的任何目的(如广告、画像、信贷评估等)。

### 5. 儿童隐私

由于本扩展不收集任何数据,不会对任何年龄段的用户构成隐私风险。

### 6. 您的权利

所有数据均在您的设备上:截图可自行删除,卸载扩展即清除全部设置。因不涉及数据收集,无需向我们行使数据请求权。

### 7. 政策变更

如本政策有实质变更,将在本页面更新并修改"最后更新"日期。

## English Version

Auto Screenshot Saver ("the extension") is a Chrome browser extension that captures webpage screenshots on a schedule or on demand and saves them to your computer. **The extension collects, transmits, and sells no user data whatsoever.**

### 1. Data We Collect

**None.** The extension contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or telemetry code, and makes no requests to any developer-controlled server. All functionality runs locally on your device.

### 2. What the Extension Accesses

- **Webpage screenshots:** when you trigger a capture (manually or on a schedule), the extension uses the browser's screenshot API to capture the current page and **saves it directly to your computer's Downloads folder**. Screenshots never leave your device; the developer has no access to them.
- **Page geometry & scrolling:** in full-page mode, an injected script reads page height and scrolls the page solely to stitch the screenshot together. Page content itself is never read or uploaded.
- **Tab information:** the URL and title of a tab are read only to generate the screenshot's file name. Nothing is logged or transmitted.
- **Settings:** your preferences (interval, format, naming pattern, etc.) are stored in `chrome.storage`. If you have Chrome Sync enabled, settings may sync through your own Google account under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy); the developer cannot access this data.

### 3. Storage & Retention

Screenshots are stored in your local Downloads folder under your full control and can be deleted at any time. Uninstalling the extension removes all of its settings. The developer retains no user data.

### 4. Third Parties & Data Sales

The extension shares no data with third parties, bundles no third-party SDKs, sells no data, and never uses data for purposes unrelated to its core functionality (such as advertising, profiling, or credit scoring).

### 5. Children's Privacy

Because the extension collects no data, it poses no privacy risk to users of any age.

### 6. Your Rights

All data lives on your device: delete screenshots yourself at any time; uninstalling clears all settings. Since no data is collected, no data requests to us are necessary.

If this policy changes materially, this page will be updated along with the "Last updated" date.
